How to read the board
Model P%
Estimated probability this player scores the first touchdown.
Fair odds
What the odds would be if the model probability were priced perfectly.
Book odds
The sportsbook price we're comparing against.
Edge
Model probability minus sportsbook implied probability. Bigger positive = better.
EV / $1
Estimated long-run profit per $1 if the model probability is correct.
BET / LEAN / PASS
BET clears the strict threshold. LEAN is interesting but weaker. PASS means the price isn't good enough.
